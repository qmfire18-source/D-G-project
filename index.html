01 — Context & Key Tensions

The Structural Paradox
of Luxury Beauty

Luxury beauty brands operate under a fundamental structural tension: they must drive sustained commercial performance while protecting an image built on exclusivity, desirability, and long-term emotional value. Fully internalised in 2021 following the end of its licensing agreement with Shiseido, DGB now manages its own operations across four subsidiaries and approximately 500 employees, generating €660 million in revenue in 2025 — a 32% increase since 2019. The Middle East has emerged as a strategic priority: valued at $15B in 2023 and projected to double by 2030, driven by high purchasing power, a young digital-native population, and deep cultural attachment to beauty, gifting, and fragrance.

Six Identified Tensions

Visibility vs. Prestige
Discounts drive conversion but directly erode luxury prestige cues — DGB's zero-discount policy is correct but creates disadvantage during key retail moments.
Experience vs. Transaction
Luxury brands must resist mass-retail logic on owned channels — elevating every purchase into an editorial experience, not a frictionless checkout.
Exclusivity vs. Accessibility
Growth must come from extending the perception of exclusivity — not from genuinely making products less rare or widely accessible.
Loyalty Gap
DGB has no formal loyalty programme — its most significant competitive gap vs. Dior Beauty Privé, Chanel La Collection, and YSL Beauty Club.
Brand Control vs. Retail Dependency
Retailers drive volume but introduce visible discounts, standardised presentation, and reduced storytelling control — a structural vulnerability.
Personalisation vs. Authenticity
AI-driven mass personalisation is becoming ubiquitous — making products feel algorithmic. Luxury must use data to create genuine rarity signals, not broadcast relevance.

"In luxury, value must be added, not reduced — the most successful players aestheticise commerce, making it invisible behind gifting, exclusivity, and storytelling."

03 — Promotional Mechanics

A Categorised Analysis

Five promotional mechanics identified and assessed for their compatibility with luxury brand equity — from safest to highest-risk.

🎁
Gift with Purchase
✓ Gold Standard

Shifts focus from price to value. Increases perceived exclusivity and basket size without markdown language. Universally adopted across the luxury set.

D&G: Pre-launch miniatures — receive sample before official release. "First to discover" positioning.
💎
Limited Editions
✓ Scarcity Lever

Creates urgency and exclusivity without price reference. Hermès model: scarcity IS the promotion. Must be genuine and meaningful to remain effective.

D&G: Online-exclusive drops — products/bundles not yet available at any retailer.
⭐
Loyalty Programs
⚡ Strategic Gap

Compatible with prestige only when access-based — not points or discounts. Dior Beauty Privé, Chanel La Collection. DGB has no programme yet.

Priority: "Private Client Circle" — access-based tiers with 1:1 WhatsApp concierge, early drops, exclusive Ramadan/Eid events.
🛍
Retailer-led Activation
✓ Structural Separation

Retailers absorb commercial pressure that brands cannot apply to owned channels. Sephora, Ounass, Chalhoub Group play this role in the ME.

Architecture: DGB.com = prestige-pure. Retail partners = volume & conversion engine.
⚠
Discount Visibility
⚠ Minimize

Highest-risk mechanic. Visible markdowns contradict exclusivity signals, reduce perceived value, and condition customers to wait for promotions.

D&G: Zero-discount policy on DGB.com ✓ — fully aligned with Hermès / Chanel / Dior best practice.

05 — Tensions

Commercial Push vs. Brand Equity

Six structural tensions that DGB must actively navigate — each represents a competing set of imperatives that cannot be fully resolved, only managed through strategic governance.

Visibility ⚡ Prestige
Explicit promotions drive short-term conversion but erode luxury perception. DGB's zero-discount policy is correct but reduces competitive visibility during Black Friday.
Experience ⚡ Transaction
Luxury brands must resist frictionless-checkout logic on owned channels, instead elevating every purchase into a brand experience with storytelling and premium presentation.
Exclusivity ⚡ Accessibility
Growth must come from extending the perception of exclusivity — not from genuinely making products less rare. More consumers must feel privileged, not see lower prices.
Brand ⚡ Retailers
Retailers drive acquisition and volume but introduce brand risk: visible markdowns, standardised presentation, and reduced narrative control — a structural vulnerability.
Short-term ⚡ Long-term CLV
Promotional cycles condition customers to wait for discounts, weakening margins over time. Brand equity investment builds customer lifetime value more sustainably.
Personalisation ⚡ Authenticity
AI-driven mass personalisation is now ubiquitous — products feel algorithmic rather than crafted. Luxury brands must use data to create genuine rarity, not broadcast relevance.
06 — Strategic Recommendation Framework

The Ideal Digital Strategy
for D&G Beauty

✓
What To Do
▸Private Client Circle — Status-based access ecosystem: 1:1 WhatsApp concierge and personal advisors, invitation-only previews, early access to launches, private online sales, exclusive Ramadan and Eid events. Social capital over transactional value.
▸Limited Edition Digital Drops — Timed online releases, region-specific launches tied to Ramadan and Eid, digital storytelling with livestream reveals. Scarcity as the mechanic, not price.
▸Digital-to-Retail Luxury Experiences — Online booking for in-store consultations, livestreamed beauty masterclasses, VIP event RSVPs tied to digital engagement, integration with mall-based luxury environments.
✗
What To Avoid
✗ Visible Discounting
–% OFF messages on any owned channel
–Strike-through pricing
–Aggressive promotional banners
–Cheap bundling — random sets without brand narrative
Trains customers to wait for price drops — permanently erodes perceived value and pricing power.
✗ Mass Market Loyalty Mechanics
–Points-based systems & generic rewards
–Automated, non-personalised clienteling blasts
–Same communication to all customer tiers
–Overusing limited editions — scarcity only works when it's genuine
Loyalty must feel like a privilege, not a programme. Mass mechanics destroy the exclusivity signal entirely.
✗ Forgetting Localisation
–One-size-fits-all global execution
–Lack of cultural alignment
–Purely Western-centric messaging or timing
ME consumers expect Ramadan, Eid, and gifting culture to be central — not an afterthought of a global calendar.
⚖
Governance Principles
▸01 — Prestige Preservation Every activation must reinforce exclusivity, desirability & storytelling. Commercial intent must exist but never be visible.
▸02 — Invisible Commerciality D&G must generate revenue — but the mechanism must be invisible. Aestheticise: gift it, limit it, personalise it.
▸03 — Access Over Discount Value delivered through privilege, experience & access — never through price reduction on any owned touchpoint.
▸04 — Cultural Relevance All ME activations must reflect Ramadan, Eid, regional gifting culture & fragrance traditions. No copy-paste Western strategy.
▸05 — Controlled Distribution Strict separation: owned = experience-first; retailers = volume-first. This dual architecture must be actively governed.
▸06 — Data as Luxury Tool First-party data enables hyper-personalised access orchestration — not mass promotional broadcasts to anonymous lists.
▸07 — Verifiable Transparency Avoid vague sustainability claims. Emphasise verifiable sourcing, ingredient traceability, and product quality — no greenwashing.
▸08 — Local Compliance & Modesty All Middle East activations must respect Ramadan etiquette, local modesty norms, and regional regulatory requirements. No Western defaults.
🛍
Retailer Differentiation
▸DGB.com (owned) Zero discounts. Exclusive VIP drops, pre-launch access, Private Client Circle, premium gifting, full editorial storytelling & brand control.
▸Sephora ME Key retail partner for customer acquisition. Allow Beauty Insider mechanics. Activate Ramadan campaigns, seasonal GWP & commercial moments.
▸Ounass Primary ME digital luxury platform. Premium tone maintained. Visible markdowns & Amber loyalty programme activation acceptable at retailer level.
▸LookFantastic Digital acquisition engine. Aggressive promotions acceptable — drives new customer trial and introduces D&G to discovery-led audience.
▸Chalhoub Group Dominant ME luxury distributor — UAE, KSA, Qatar, Kuwait. Strategic partnership essential for physical retail access across the region.
▸Retailers drive short-term volume — D&G builds long-term desirability. Strong brand equity makes retail activation more effective over time.
